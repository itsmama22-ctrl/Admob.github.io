MuteLock Support

Welcome to the official support page for MuteLock. We are here to help you stay focused and productive. If you run into issues or have questions, please review the sections below.

Contact

Email: mutelock.app@gmail.com

Common Questions

  • Session failed unexpectedly? Leaving the app, locking your phone, or opening Control Center will fail the session. This is intentional to help you stay focused.
  • Ads showing too frequently? We use Google AdMob. Ads only appear when opening the dashboard or when a session ends. They help keep the app free.
  • Notes missing after reinstall? Session history and notes are stored locally. Deleting the app removes them permanently.

Privacy

MuteLock does not collect personal data. Session history and notes stay on your device. We use Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect anonymous data to display relevant ads. View the full privacy policy here.
