Privacy Policy for MuteLock

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

MuteLock ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Data Collection

MuteLock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • All session data (timer settings, notes, session history) is stored locally on your device only
  • We do not have access to your data
  • No user accounts or login required
  • No data is transmitted to our servers

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

MuteLock uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect information for ad personalization and measurement purposes, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • App usage data
  • Location data (approximate)
  • Advertising data

For details about AdMob's data practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking on iOS).

Data Storage

  • All app data is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and Core Data
  • No data is transmitted to external servers (except ad requests handled by AdMob)
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data

Children's Privacy

MuteLock is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app
  • No account or login required - complete control over your data
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
